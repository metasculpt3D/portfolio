## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added

- Initial scaffold: `index.html`, `404.html`, and empty `favicon.ico`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and `site.webmanifest`.
- Design system in `assets/css/`: tokens in `base.css`, layout, and components, loaded through `main.css`.
- Portfolio page with a skip link, header, hero, services, work, about, contact, and footer.
- CSS motion: hero entrance, scroll reveal, hover lift, and the mobile menu slide.
- Documentation: README, design system, content guide, launch checklist, and architecture.
